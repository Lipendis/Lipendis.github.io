1. Who we are

Lipendis Studios is an independent game developer based in the Republic of Turkey. For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and similar laws, Lipendis Studios is the data controller for the personal information described in this Policy, except where a third-party service acts as an independent controller (see section 6). You can reach us at LipendisCompany@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Support and correspondence: your email address, name and anything you write when you contact us, including screenshots or attachments you choose to send.
  • In-game profile: a player name or avatar you choose. In Games without online features these stay on your device.
  • Account information: if a Game offers an account or sign-in, the identifier and basic profile information from the sign-in provider you choose (for example Google Play Games, Apple Game Center or Sign in with Apple), such as a player ID and display name.
  • Content you create: if a Game lets players communicate or share content, the messages, names and content you submit.
  • Surveys and feedback: any answers you choose to give.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, where you permit it), vendor or app-instance identifiers (such as IDFV or a Firebase installation ID), and identifiers created by our Games or partners.
  • Device and connection information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and carrier, and IP address.
  • Approximate location: country, region or city derived from your IP address. Our Games do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Gameplay and usage data: your progress, levels, scores, settings, in-game currency and items, session times and lengths, and how you interact with Game features.
  • Purchase information: the products you buy, their price and currency, and transaction identifiers. Payment details are handled by the app store and are never shared with us.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown, whether you watched, tapped or installed from them, and related measurement data.
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports, error logs and performance data.
  • Notification tokens: if a Game sends push notifications and you allow them, a token that lets the notification service reach your device.

Information from third parties

  • App stores and platforms (Apple, Google and others) may tell us about purchases, refunds and sign-ins.
  • Advertising and attribution partners may tell us how you came to install a Game and how ads performed.

Our Games do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location unless a specific feature needs it, in which case your device will ask for your permission first and you can refuse.

3. How we use information

  • to provide, run and save the Games, including your progress, purchases and settings;
  • to process and restore purchases and prevent purchases being granted twice;
  • to show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to measure them;
  • to understand how the Games are played, and to fix, balance and improve them;
  • to diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • to provide online features such as accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards and multiplayer;
  • to send notifications you have allowed;
  • to answer your questions and support requests;
  • to detect and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse and security incidents;
  • to comply with the law, enforce our Terms of Service and protect our rights and those of our players.

We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR, KVKK or a similar law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Games and the purchases you make.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Games working, secure and fair, to understand and improve them, and to fund free Games through advertising, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — where the law requires it, for example for certain personalised advertising, tracking on iOS, and push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that happened before.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records the law requires, such as tax records for purchases, and to respond to lawful requests.

Under KVKK, these correspond to the grounds in Article 5 of the law, including the establishment or performance of a contract, our legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations and, where required, your explicit consent.

5. Advertising

Many of our Games are free and are funded by advertising. Ads are delivered by advertising networks and mediation platforms (listed in section 6) that collect information directly from your device, including your advertising identifier, IP address, device information and ad interactions, to choose, deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud.

Ads may be personalised, based on your interests as inferred from your activity in our Games and other apps, or non-personalised, based only on general information such as the Game being played and your approximate location. You can limit personalised ads:

  • iOS: a Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads (the path varies by device).
  • Industry tools: you can also use the opt-out tools of the Digital Advertising Alliance (AppChoices) or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (Your Online Choices).

Opting out does not stop ads; it means the ads you see are less relevant to you. Some Games offer a purchase that removes some or all ads.

7. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that host, run or support the Games on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the information.
  • Advertising, analytics and attribution partners, as described in sections 5 and 6. Under some US state laws, letting these partners collect information for personalised ("cross-context behavioural") advertising can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; see section 12 for how to opt out.
  • App stores and platforms, to process purchases, sign-ins and reviews.
  • Other players, where a Game has online features — for example your player name and scores on a leaderboard.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, court order or a lawful request from public authorities, or to protect the rights, safety and property of players, the public or us.
  • Business transfers, if Lipendis Studios or a Game is sold, merged or reorganised, in which case information may pass to the new owner under this Policy.
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

8. International transfers

We are based in Turkey, and many of the services we use are operated from or store data in other countries, including the United States and member states of the European Union. Those countries may not offer the same level of protection as yours. Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or KVKK requires it, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the standard contracts and procedures set out in Article 9 of KVKK, or another lawful transfer mechanism, or with your explicit consent where the law requires it.

9. How long we keep information

  • Data stored only on your device stays there until you delete the Game or its data.
  • Account and cloud-save data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete it or the account is closed, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Support emails are kept for up to two years after the conversation ends.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Analytics and diagnostic data is kept in identifiable form for no longer than 26 months.
  • Third-party services keep information for the periods set out in their own policies.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including relying on reputable service providers and limiting who can access data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • learn whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and for direct marketing;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email LipendisCompany@gmail.com and tell us which Game you play and what you are asking for. We may ask for information to confirm the request is yours, such as an in-game player ID. We answer within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. You can also use the device settings in section 5 at any time.

Because many of our Games store data only on your device and we hold no account for you, we may not be able to link a request to any data. In that case we will tell you, and will help you reach the third-party services that do hold data.

12. Region-specific information

Turkey (KVKK)

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about that processing; to learn its purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad it is transferred to; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction under Article 7; to request that third parties to whom the data was transferred are notified of a correction or deletion; to object to an unfavourable result arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing. Send applications to the email address above; we respond free of charge within 30 days. You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You have the rights listed in section 11 under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority where you live or work.

United States

If you live in California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to access, correct and delete it; and to opt out of its sale, its sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, and targeted advertising. In the past 12 months the categories we may have collected are identifiers, commercial information (purchases), internet or network activity (gameplay and ad interactions), and approximate geolocation, from the sources and for the purposes described above. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

To opt out of sale or sharing, turn off your device's advertising identifier as described in section 5, or email us. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal, where a Game or our website can detect one, as a valid opt-out request. You may use an authorised agent to submit a request, and if we deny a request you may appeal by replying to our answer.

Brazil

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) you have the rights listed in section 11 and may complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

Other countries

If you live elsewhere, including Canada, Japan, South Korea, Australia or Indonesia, you have the rights your local law gives you, and you can exercise them by contacting us.

13. Deleting your data

  • Data on your device: uninstall the Game, or clear its data in your device settings. This permanently deletes local progress.
  • Accounts and cloud saves: use the account-deletion option inside the Game where there is one, or email us with your player ID and the Game's name, and we will delete the account and its associated data within 30 days.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and contact the relevant third-party service listed in section 6.

14. Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through a Game, contact us and we will delete it and take steps to stop further collection. If we learn that a player is under the applicable age, we will limit or delete the information associated with them.
