Antillery Privacy Policy

Lipendis Studios · Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Antillery (the "Game"), made by Lipendis Studios ("we", "us"). The short version: we do not run any servers, we do not have player accounts, and we do not collect personal information ourselves. The Game shows ads, and the ad services it uses collect some information from your device, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, the player name you choose and a record of your recent purchases are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data, and we cannot recover it for you.

Advertising

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads and ads between runs, through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads, both operated by Unity Technologies. To show and measure ads, these services may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS);
  • your IP address, and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and network type;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information goes directly to the ad services, not to us. It is used for ad delivery, personalised advertising, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Unity's handling of it is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The Game receives only the product bought and a transaction identifier, which it keeps on your device so a purchase is never granted twice and can be restored.

Store reviews

The Game may show the store's own rating prompt. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google or Apple under their own policies.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow ad services to collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and you would like help with their data, contact us at the address below.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we hold no personal information about you, the data in question is held by the ad services; you can exercise your rights with Unity directly through its privacy policy, limit it through the device settings above, or contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds a new service. The effective date above will change when we do.
